Q.10 · The Maze · faithful mock in the current theme

Fully playable. Steer the tiny dot with WASD or the arrow keys through a 17x13 maze generated fresh on every load: touch a wall and you are rewound about 1.5 seconds along your own path. A 45 second timer runs the whole time, when it expires you lose a heart and the timer resets, your position is untouched. Reach the glowing exit at the top edge to pass. The pause button in the paper header works: it freezes the timer and the dot. Theme restyles versus the real build: walls are ink on the exam-paper panel, the player dot is oxblood with a deep oxblood stroke instead of arcade blue, the exit glow is a pulsing pass green instead of bright arcade green, the timer bar is flat pass green flipping to danger red instead of a rainbow hue sweep, the wall-hit "oof" sound is stood in for by a brief red flash of the dot, and the controls hint is reworded and sits just above the bar. The entry triangle is unchanged from the real build. Nothing off-palette.
