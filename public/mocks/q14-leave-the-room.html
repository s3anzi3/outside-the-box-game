Q.14 · Leave The Room · proposed replacement for mirrored text

Fully playable. A proctor's eye inked on the exam paper follows your cursor everywhere, blinking now and then, with a pulsing PROCTOR STATUS: WATCHING readout beneath it. Clicking ANSWER while observed always slams INCORRECT and costs a heart, and the examiner's rebukes escalate: the third one lets slip "...in this window, anyway," which is the hint. The solve: actually leave. Switch tabs, click another window, or minimize for about four seconds; the game notices the window losing focus, and when you come back the paper has been stamped in your absence and the examiner is mid-meltdown. The trap only arms after you have interacted with the page, so switching back to this tab from elsewhere does not auto-win before you have even played. Tunables flagged for the port: the four second away threshold, and the same blur plus visibilitychange events work identically in the real canvas build.
