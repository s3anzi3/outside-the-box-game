Fully playable. FAITHFUL PORT. The exam paper turns into an arena: the play area tints pale pink (near black after hours), the header sprouts scanlines and the Q.28 label twitches and corrupts, and a black monitor with phosphor eyes and a jagged mouth sweeps back and forth under a name tag reading FRODRICK.EXE. You are the small ink-blue ship at the bottom: A/D or the arrows move, SPACE fires, hold it to autofire. Every number is the real build's: 110 boss HP and 5 damage a hit, so 22 hits to kill; phases at 75 and 40 HP that speed his sweep from 0.45 to 0.72 to 1.10 Hz and change his fire from a single drop to a triple to a five-way fan (275, 360, 440 px/s); 0.42 s of blinking invincibility after a hit. There is no lateral trick, and the real build has none either: this is the Act III set piece, an honest bullet-dodge between two trick questions. The trap is standing under him when he fires. Each shot that lands slams INCORRECT and costs a heart; three and the Proctor voids you. Restyled to the theme only: the neon phase colours become ink green, yellow and red, the ship is ink blue, and in light mode the phase-change flash tints in the new phase colour because a white flash on pale paper is invisible. The examiner adds four short lines (phase one, phase two, first hit, second hit) as a hint ladder; the real build has only the opening remark. Pause freezes the whole loop. Caveats: no sound; the header glitch is a mock addition the brief asked for; the fight is real and takes a minute or two.
