Q.15 · Outside the Box · connect-the-pairs redesign of the title level

Fully playable, v4. Lines are now real freehand pencil strokes, not grid paths: click a seal and the line follows your cursor like a pen (no button-holding needed, so it works on a touchpad; press-and-drag also works), and it completes the moment it reaches the matching seal. Clicking anywhere mid-stroke abandons the line. If your line bumps another line, or another color's seal, the stamp slams INCORRECT, you LOSE A HEART, and the stroke is thrown out. Red's seals sit flush against the left and right paper edges and blue's flush against the top and bottom, so whichever of the two is drawn first becomes an edge-to-edge wall: the second provably cannot be completed inside the paper, no gaps to squeeze through. The way out stays invisible: grab the pristine top-left corner and pull; it peels, then tears off, taking the Q.15 label with it, and from then on lines may leave the paper and route around the outside across the desk. The examiner gives NO hints: he states the rules once, reacts only after you rip the corner or draw on the desk, and otherwise stays silent. The discovery is entirely yours. RESET clears lines but never restores the corner or hearts.
