Q.27 · Keys But No Locks · FAITHFUL PORT

Fully playable. The paper prints the four-line riddle in italic serif: keys but no locks, space but no rooms, you can enter but cannot go inside, what am I? Under it sit four answer buttons, HOUSE, MAP, PIANO and KEYBOARD, and every one of them slams INCORRECT and costs a heart, KEYBOARD included. The examiner's opening line is the real Q27 copy and it is the whole hint: the answer is the means by which you give it. Type K-E-Y-B-O-A-R-D on your physical keyboard and the level passes on the D. The letters you type appear faintly at the bottom of the paper as you go, Backspace erases one, and the buffer only ever keeps your last eight letters, exactly as in the real build. Clicking the KEYBOARD button earns "Yes. That is the word. That is not how you say it." and a second miss on any button earns "The answer is not on the paper, candidate. It is under your hands." Keys are ignored while the exam is paused. New for the mock: the small ANSWER IN THE SPACE PROVIDED directive above the riddle (the space bar is, technically, provided), a first-miss line for the other three buttons, and a one-time "Keep going." reaction when the first letter lands. Caveat: keystrokes go wherever the browser's focus is, so on the review board click the paper once before typing if the letters do not appear.
