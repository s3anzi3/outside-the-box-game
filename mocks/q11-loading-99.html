Q.11 · Loading… 99% · faithful mock in the current theme

Fully playable. The exam "compiles your results": the bar loads with a believable, stuttering rhythm, then freezes at 99% forever. Waiting does nothing, ever. About three seconds after it sticks, an embossed RETRY button fades in: clicking it slams INCORRECT, costs a heart, and starts the load over from 0%. Flagging RETRY as a proposed addition: the level's header comment describes it, but the shipped code never draws the button. The real answer is to grab the handle at the edge of the fill (it turns antique gold once the bar is stuck) and drag the last 1% across the line yourself. The green 100% stamp on completion is a mock-only flourish, the real level cuts straight to the win screen. Note: Q.46 later quizzes the player on how they beat this level, so the drag mechanic must stay memorable.
