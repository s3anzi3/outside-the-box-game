Q.20 · System Breach · faithful mock in the current theme

Fully playable. The Act II finale: the exam is glitching as something forces its way in. The banner jitters, the paper itself twitches every few seconds, and the exam offers you two sanctioned responses to "Terminate the intruder?": YES and NO. Both are traps; both slam INCORRECT and cost a heart. The only way out flickers in the paper's bottom-right corner: a dark ⛔ OVERRIDE button, the one control the Institute would never sanction. Pressing it wins: "ACCESS OVERRIDDEN. You broke the one rule they gave you. Sometimes that is the only way out." This plants Q21's "maybe this time you need to cheat" Frodrick rematch. Faithful to the real build; the flicker is cosmetic and the button stays clickable through its off frames so it never feels unfair.
